    Stop 2 of 6

    Badges and the Top 40

    Every participant can earn the micro-credential. Learners from partner organizations can also be selected as one of 40 finalists who get extra support as they move into their careers.

    Badge vs. Top 40

    Everyone who completes all coursework and submits a portfolio receives their micro-credential badge, but only learners from partner organizations are eligible for the Top 40 cohort.

    How finalists are chosen
    • 01
      Lessons

      Completion and quality of your lessons.

    • 02
      Portfolio

      Creativity and content of your portfolio.

    • 03
      Community

      Engagement in the Slack community.

    What finalists get
    • 01
      Expert career coaching

      One step beyond the Plato coach.

    • 02
      Employment connections

      Introductions to employers who are hiring.

    • 03
      Graduation networking event

      Opportunities to attend and meet employers. More perks will be announced closer to graduation.

    When

    Selection happens near the end of the cohort, before the program wraps up. Keep your lessons and portfolio moving so your work is ready to review.

    Tip

    Engagement starts on day one. Post your intro in Slack, answer a poll, and reply to a classmate. Small, steady participation adds up.

    Your portfolio is your main proof of skill, and you'll present it as the program wraps up. It's scored on the six criteria below. Tick them off to preview the standard.

    Stop 5 of 6

    Slack PLCs: meet your small group

    With a very small team and a very large cohort, we're trying something new: Professional Learning Communities (PLCs). We split into five smaller groups so everyone gets a close-knit community experience.

    Four PLCs give a more targeted experience for specialized areas. The fifth, the Gumbo group, is for anyone who wants a highly mixed learning environment. Fun fact: gumbo is a Louisiana dish known for a mix of ingredients that come together to make an amazing stew.

    You choose your group

    There are no restrictions. If you're a faculty member who wants to see how freshmen and sophomores engage with AI, join that group. If you're not a computer science major but want to push your technical skills to the limit, join Advanced Tech Skills.

    01

    Freshmen/Sophomores

    Looking for internships and still building your resume and LinkedIn.

    02

    Educators

    Staff, faculty, and Education majors.

    03

    Upperclassmen/Professionals

    Juniors, seniors, grad students, and professionals outside of education.

    04

    Advanced Tech Skills

    CS majors, developers, and anyone with advanced tech skills.

    05

    Gumbo

    Can't decide, or just want to mix it up? This one's for you.

    Tap a channel name to copy it, then paste it into Slack's search bar.

    The one rule
    Pick ONE group and stick with it for the whole cohort.

    Other than that, there are no restrictions. Choose wisely, and join your channel today.

    Your PLC is home base

    Once you join your PLC channel, that's where most of your interaction will happen. Share anything program-related right there.

    You're still welcome to network and chat in #ai-leaders and #ai-leaders-community, but those channels aren't official.

    Mandatory for everyone

    Also join #ai-leaders-official-announcements. That's where official program updates are posted.

    How to join a channel

    Desktop or web

    1. In the sidebar, click Channels, then Browse channels. You can also type the channel name in the search bar at the top.
    2. Click the channel you want.
    3. Click Join Channel at the bottom of the screen.

    Mobile

    1. Tap Home, then + next to Channels.
    2. Tap Browse channels.
    3. Tap the channel, then tap Join.
