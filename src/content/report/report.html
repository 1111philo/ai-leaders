plato: how the agents work together

Named for the first computerized tutor, plato combines an LMS with the ease of a chatbot. After 12 prototypes, the Cohort 1 pilot ran 10 agents that apply learning science and personalize each path, with educators in the loop. Six of them:

Educator builds the course →
1 · Educator
Knowledge Base Editor

Trains every agent on the syllabus, course map and materials.

2 · Educator
Lesson Creator

Makes objectives measurable; ties each lesson to an Exemplar project and rubric.

3 · System
Lesson Extractor

Turns each lesson into parameters the Coach can use.

4 · Learner
Coach

Guides each Learner to mastery. Instructs, encourages and assesses; never does the work for them.

Learner and platform improve over time ↺
Feeds the Coach
Learner Profile Owner

Builds a profile of interests, goals and needs from direct input and observed performance. It gets stronger with use.

Improves plato
Pilot

Turns user feedback into code changes automatically.

01Program overview

The AI Leaders program is a free, remote micro-credential designed to upskill participants in AI leadership and prepare them to land living-wage jobs. The virtual course is built for busy students and professionals and is designed to be completed in 12 weeks or less, totaling approximately 20 hours of instruction.

02Learning platform: plato

The course is hosted on plato, an open source, AI-powered microlearning platform.

Format
Conversational lessons

Lessons are continuous, interactive conversations with an AI coach.

Pace
About 20 minutes each

Each lesson covers 2 to 4 learning objectives in about 11 exchanges.

Assessment
Mastery-based

The AI evaluates your text or image submissions and tracks progress toward a mastery-level exemplar.

03Requirements & incentives

Required
WordPress Portfolio

All graduates build and showcase a WordPress-powered portfolio as proof of completion.

Incentive
Top 40 Finalists

High-performing graduates seeking employment receive expert career coaching, letters of recommendation and direct connections to hiring employers. Finalists are chosen on the quality of their work and their community engagement.

04Six portfolio criteria

  1. Skillful AI useArtifacts showing skillful generative and agentic AI use for efficiency, ideation, iteration or experimentation
  2. AI ethicsConcisely explains and demonstrates your personal approach to AI ethics
  3. Deployed WordPress siteMultiple pages using multiple WordPress design elements and features
  4. WordPress AIUses current WordPress AI capabilities
  5. About youPersonal introduction, resume, work artifacts and visuals demonstrating your skills and goals
  6. Professional designVisuals, design and organization support your goals and align to professional standards in your field
Each criterion is equally weighted. Your portfolio needs to demonstrate all six clearly: not just touch them, but show evidence of skill and intentionality in each area.

06Strategies for success & pacing

  • 01
    Manage the AI coachThe plato AI coach aims to guide you to the lesson's target exemplar in around 11 exchanges. Keep your prompts focused on the specific learning objectives to move through lessons efficiently.
  • 02
    Prioritize the technical portfolioYour final WordPress site is your primary proof of capability. Apply concepts from the WordPress module early, and dedicate time to iteratively refine your deployment, custom styling and content structure.
  • 03
    Engage for the Top 40Because the Top 40 spots offer significant career coaching and networking benefits, focus on your portfolio's quality and participate actively in the community from week one.
